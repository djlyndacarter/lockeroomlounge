About The Lockeroom

Since 1985, The Lockeroom has been a historic sports bar and food truck in Detroit's University District, just off the Avenue of Fashion.

We've recently remodeled into an indoor/outdoor sports lounge with 25 flat-screen TVs and interactive games, plus a Hall of Fame honoring local and national sports heroes.

Our street-food menu is made to enjoy in the lounge or take to go, with sports-themed favorites like the Red Wings and the Stanley Cup. At the bar, order a drink named for a Detroit legend or landmark, like the Miguel Cabrera or the Belle Island.

Serving you is our favorite sport!
