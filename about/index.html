About

The LOCKEROOM

Since 1985, The LOCKEROOM has been a historic sports bar and food truck in Detroit's University District, just off the Avenue of Fashion. Recently remodeled into an indoor/outdoor sports lounge, it features 25 flat-screen TVs, interactive games and a "Hall of Fame" honoring local and national sports heroes.

Our street-food menu is made to enjoy in the lounge or take to go, with sports-themed favorites like the Red Wings and the Stanley Cup. At the bar, order a drink named for a Detroit legend or landmark, like the Miguel Cabrera or the Belle Island. Serving You is our Favorite Sport!
