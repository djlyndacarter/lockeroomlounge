SMS Terms

The Lockeroom Lounge & Food Truck may send text messages to customers who opt in. You can reach us by text or phone at 1-877-313-3552.

Messages you'll receive

Reminders and follow-ups about your bookings and visits. Expect up to 2 messages per week.

Opting in

You opt in by texting us first at 1-877-313-3552, or by checking the SMS box on one of our forms. Leave that box unchecked if you don't want texts.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or contact us and we'll remove you. Reply HELP for help, or email lockeroomlounge@gmail.com.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier and plan.

Your information

Information collected when you consent to SMS will not be shared with third parties for their marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
